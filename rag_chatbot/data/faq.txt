FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS — NORTHWIND TECH SOLUTIONS

Q: How do I request time off?
A: Submit a request through the HR Portal under "Leave Requests." Your
manager will be notified automatically and must approve it within 3 business
days.

Q: Who do I contact for IT issues?
A: Open a ticket at helpdesk.northwindtech.example or email
it-support@northwindtech.example. For urgent issues, call the IT hotline at
extension 4400.

Q: How do I reset my company password?
A: Go to the Single Sign-On portal and click "Forgot Password." You will
receive a reset link on your registered company email. Passwords must be at
least 12 characters and changed every 90 days.

Q: What is the process for expense reimbursement?
A: Log into the Expense Portal, upload your receipts, categorize the expense,
and submit for manager approval. Reimbursements are processed within 10
business days of approval.

Q: Can I work from home permanently?
A: Fully remote arrangements are evaluated on a case-by-case basis and
require approval from both your manager and HR. The standard policy allows
up to 2 remote days per week without special approval.

Q: How do I enroll in health benefits?
A: New employees have 30 days from their start date to enroll in health
benefits through the Benefits Portal. After this window, enrollment is only
possible during the annual open enrollment period or after a qualifying life
event.

Q: Where can I find the employee handbook?
A: The full employee handbook is available on the internal wiki under
Company > HR > Handbook.

Q: Who approves new software purchases?
A: Software purchases under $200/year can be approved by your direct
manager. Purchases above that amount require approval from the department
head and a review by IT Security.

Q: How do I report a workplace concern?
A: You can email hr@northwindtech.example, speak to your manager, or use the
anonymous ethics hotline listed on the HR portal homepage.

Q: What is the notice period for resignation?
A: The standard notice period is 4 weeks for individual contributors and 8
weeks for managers and above, unless otherwise specified in your employment
contract.
