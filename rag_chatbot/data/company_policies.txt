COMPANY POLICIES — NORTHWIND TECH SOLUTIONS

1. WORK HOURS
Standard work hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with a one-hour
lunch break. Employees may request flexible start times between 7:30 AM and
10:00 AM with manager approval.

2. REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees are permitted to work remotely up to 2 days per week. Requests for
additional remote days must be approved by the department head. All remote
employees must be reachable via Slack and email during core hours (10 AM–4 PM).

3. LEAVE POLICY
- Annual Leave: 20 paid days per year, accrued monthly.
- Sick Leave: 10 paid days per year. A medical certificate is required for
  absences longer than 2 consecutive days.
- Parental Leave: 16 weeks paid leave for primary caregivers, 4 weeks for
  secondary caregivers.
- Unused annual leave can be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the
  next calendar year.

4. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
Employees must submit expense claims through the internal Expense Portal
within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims must include original
receipts. Reimbursable categories include travel, client meals (up to $75 per
person), and approved software subscriptions.

5. IT & SECURITY POLICY
All company laptops must have full-disk encryption and the company VPN client
installed. Passwords must be changed every 90 days and must be at least 12
characters long. Two-factor authentication is mandatory for all company
accounts, including email and the HR portal.

6. CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with
respect and professionalism. Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of
any kind will not be tolerated and should be reported to HR immediately via
hr@northwindtech.example or the anonymous ethics hotline.

7. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in June and December. Reviews
include a self-assessment, manager assessment, and peer feedback (360-degree
review) for employees at the senior level and above.

8. EQUIPMENT POLICY
New employees receive a standard equipment kit: laptop, monitor, keyboard,
mouse, and headset. Equipment requests outside the standard kit require
manager approval and must be submitted through the IT Helpdesk portal.

9. TRAVEL POLICY
Domestic flights should be booked in economy class. International flights
longer than 6 hours may be booked in premium economy. All travel must be
booked through the approved corporate travel agency, TravelWise Corporate.

10. OFFBOARDING
Departing employees must return all company equipment within 5 business days
of their last working day. IT access is revoked at 6:00 PM on the last working
day. A final paycheck, including any accrued and unused leave, is issued
within 2 pay cycles.
